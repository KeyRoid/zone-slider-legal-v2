1. No Account

Zone Slider does not require account registration.

2. Core App Data

The core time zone conversion feature runs on the device.

The app may read the device time zone to initialize the default time display. This is used only to support time zone functionality.

The app does not save the last selected city pair, active city, slider position, or day shift in v1.

3. Data Collection

Zone Slider does not intentionally collect personal information through the core app experience.

If you contact support by email or another external channel, the information you send may be used to respond to your request.

Zone Slider may open the Privacy Policy link in the device browser.

Support information is provided through the app store Support URL.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, account SDKs, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchase SDKs in v1.

5. Offline Use

Core time zone conversion works offline. Opening the Privacy Policy link requires a browser and network connection.

6. Children

Zone Slider is not directed to children.
